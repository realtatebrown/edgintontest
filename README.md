# EDGINTON

A French newsweekly-inspired front page for Steven Edginton. The November 2026 edition features the Christopher Caldwell interview and links readers to Steven’s existing Substack for articles, the archive and subscriptions.

Serve the static site locally with `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory dist` and open `http://localhost:8000`.

The edition is curated in `dist/index.html`. The question is the default cover. Open `?cover=quote` for the earlier quoted headline. The lead interview, featured documentary and their timestamped links open a cinema view with an external YouTube link. Closing it returns the reader to the originating link. Film stills and fonts are served locally from `dist/assets/`.

The guest index lives in `dist/interviews.html`. Its portraits are cropped from the linked interviews. The Caldwell lead uses [Steven’s own excerpt of the exchange](https://x.com/StevenEdginton/status/2122713747647316298), with a full-height portrait cropped from the original 1920 × 1080 frame on the front page and both speakers in frame in the cinema view. The closing portrait uses the original square profile photo from [Steven’s Substack author profile](https://substack.com/@stevenedginton). The two EDGINTON wordmarks are custom lettered from Bodoni Moda, with a heavier small-size cut. The two smaller documentary stills link to 7:50 and 9:00 in the [full film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EYHWpMswzOI). The reporting on Brunswick Road is in [Steven’s account of Ron’s visit](https://www.stevenedginton.com/p/you-will-never-ever-know-what-a-lovely). The London street still comes from [Steven Edginton’s excerpt of the documentary in Tottenham](https://x.com/StevenEdginton/status/2109651289281864172).

The Army Cadets excerpt shows the original checkbox and its label from the Home Office’s [Prevent duty training, “Matthew’s story”](https://www.support-people-susceptible-to-radicalisation.service.gov.uk/case-study/awareness-course/case-studies-n/1/question). Its caption links to the course’s answer key.
