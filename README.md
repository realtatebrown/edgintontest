# EDGINTON

A French newsweekly-inspired front page for Steven Edginton. The November 2026 edition features the Christopher Caldwell interview and links readers to Steven’s existing Substack for articles, the archive and subscriptions.

Serve the static site locally with `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory dist` and open `http://localhost:8000`.

The edition is curated in `dist/index.html`. The interview player uses the YouTube watch link on the lead photograph. Film stills and fonts are served locally from `dist/assets/`.

The guest index lives in `dist/interviews.html`. Its portraits are cropped from the linked interviews. The London street still comes from [Steven Edginton’s excerpt of the documentary in Tottenham](https://x.com/StevenEdginton/status/2109651289281864172).

The Army Cadets clipping is from the Home Office’s [Prevent duty training, “Matthew’s story”](https://www.support-people-susceptible-to-radicalisation.service.gov.uk/case-study/awareness-course/case-studies-n/1/question?activity=response&skip=true).
