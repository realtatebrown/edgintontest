# EDGINTON

A French newsweekly-inspired front page for Steven Edginton. The November 2026 edition features the Christopher Caldwell interview and links readers to Steven’s existing Substack for articles, the archive and subscriptions.

Serve the static site locally with `python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory dist` and open `http://localhost:8000`.

The edition is curated in `dist/index.html`. Open `?cover=question` to compare the alternative cover headline. The lead interview, featured documentary and their timestamped links open a cinema view with an external YouTube link. Closing it returns the reader to the originating link. Film stills and fonts are served locally from `dist/assets/`.

The guest index lives in `dist/interviews.html`. Its portraits are cropped from the linked interviews. The Caldwell lead uses [Steven’s own excerpt of the exchange](https://x.com/StevenEdginton/status/2122713747647316298), with both speakers in frame on desktop and a closer crop of Caldwell on phones. The two EDGINTON wordmarks are custom lettered from Bodoni Moda, with a heavier small-size cut. The two smaller documentary stills link to 7:50 and 9:00 in the [full film](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EYHWpMswzOI). The reporting on Brunswick Road is in [Steven’s account of Ron’s visit](https://www.stevenedginton.com/p/you-will-never-ever-know-what-a-lovely). The London street still comes from [Steven Edginton’s excerpt of the documentary in Tottenham](https://x.com/StevenEdginton/status/2109651289281864172).

The Army Cadets excerpt combines the original wording and a crop of the checkbox from the Home Office’s [Prevent duty training, “Matthew’s story”](https://www.support-people-susceptible-to-radicalisation.service.gov.uk/case-study/awareness-course/case-studies-n/1/question). Its caption links to the course’s answer key.
